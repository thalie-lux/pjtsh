**Scopture** is a modular, kinetic light installation consisting of multiple fixtures called **Scopture Nodes**. It was inspired by the fast and harsh environment of professional stage lighting equipment and the cosmic vision and complexity of the James Webb Space Telescope.  
  
![Reflektoskop-1](./markdown_assets/ph-reflekt-1.jpg)  
  
A Scopture Node is an ArtNet based, PoE+ powered device with an attached mirror that is movable through three dimensions of freedom. A Scopture Node can easily be set up by connecting it to a PoE+ capable Ethernet switch, which connected to an ArtNet capable control device, can be controlled by a computer with appropriate software like GrandMA3.  
  
![Reflektoskop](./markdown_assets/ph-reflekt-2.jpg)  
  
The goal was to go through a complete production design and manufacturing process from idea to deployment according to the „Double Diamond Model”: From idea to discovery, research and prototyping, defining the solution and developing it.  
  
![Reflektoskop](./markdown_assets/ph-reflekt-4.jpg)  
  
![Reflektoskop](./markdown_assets/ph-reflekt-3.jpg)  
  
The details of this process involved the development of the Scopture Nodes involving their circuit and PCB design, programming of microcontrollers, developing the case and 3D printing, as well as the final installation and setup in an event context and the organization of the event itself.  
  
![Reflektoskop](./markdown_assets/ph-reflekt-5.jpg)  
  
That first proper deployment happened at my thesis colloquium event ["Scopture: Room - Light - Reflection"](https://www.zfk-hb.de/#scopture-raum-licht-reflexion) on 14th April 2026 at the venue NEU BAU (former "Bunker an'n Diek", "Krise") from the collective "Zentrum für Kollektivkultur" in Bremen, which was organised as a public evening of music performances.
